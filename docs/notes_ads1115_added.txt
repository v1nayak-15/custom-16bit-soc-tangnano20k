ADS1115 addition to the USB-keyboard-removed Tang Nano 20K project

Added:
- ads1115_i2c_master.v (the supplied I2C master)
- top-level inout ports ads_scl and ads_sda
- an instance of ads1115_i2c_master in top.v

Important limitations:
- The module's CPU-facing control interface is not yet memory-mapped to the Hack CPU.
  channel_sel_wr and control_wr are tied low, so the ADC will remain idle and no
  conversion will start in this integration.
- Before using the board, assign ads_scl and ads_sda to valid unused FPGA package
  pins in tangnano20k.cst for your exact Tang Nano 20K board revision.
- Use external pull-up resistors (typically 4.7 kΩ) from SDA and SCL to 3.3 V.
- Connect ADS1115 ADDR to GND for I2C address 0x48, and share ground.
- The ADS1115 I2C pins are open-drain; do not drive them directly high.
- No Gowin synthesis or physical FPGA test has been performed.

Next integration step for CPU control:
- Add memory-mapped channel-select, control/status, and data registers to the
  existing memory/computer bus, then connect their read/write strobes to this
  module. No existing CPU/GPU/HDMI/UART behavior has been intentionally changed.
